About Me

The engineer behind the numbers

I take things apart to see how they work. Then I build something better.

I was the kid who took apart an airsoft gun to see how it worked. My grandfather was an engineer at Lockheed Skunkworks. He passed before I was born, but the hand-drawn tools he left behind are a big part of why I became one.

I started as a machinist, running a HAAS 5-axis in an AS9100 aerospace shop. A month in I was lead machinist, because I kept asking why we did it that way and then built a better way. Now I’m the engineer at a plastics manufacturer, and I’m still on the floor every day.

I can’t stand watching something run inefficient when it could run right. That’s how scrap came down to under 3%, an estimated $240K a year. It’s how I ended up designing and machining fixtures that took one sheet per cycle to twenty, and setup from 60 minutes to 15. It’s also why I taught myself Python and built the tools I wished we had: a FIFO picker, ProcessForge analytics, and Floor Board, the scheduling and shop-floor execution system the plant has run on since October. I specify, direct, and validate those builds with AI as my coding partner, and I can read and change the code.

Lately that’s meant resurfacing a 48-inch cast aluminum mold in-house (40 hours of machine time down to 22, for about $600 instead of $5,000 to $15,000), tracing customer rejections to thermal expansion, and designing and machining a heat bending fixture in one week.

What I bring

Floor and machining: CNC programming and setup (HAAS 3-axis and 5-axis), CAM in Fusion 360, GD&T and print reading, fixture and tooling design, precision metrology, thermoforming, AS9100 shop experience.

Process and quality: Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, DMAIC, root cause analysis, SPC, OEE, PFMEA, Kaizen, SMED, 5S, value stream mapping, standard work, work instructions and SOPs, time studies.

Systems and software: Python (FastAPI, Streamlit, Pandas, XGBoost, SHAP), SQL and SQLite, ERP integration (SYSPRO), finite-capacity scheduling, shop-floor execution and OEE reporting, Power BI, AutoCAD.

AI in engineering: Prompt engineering with the CRIT framework (Context, Role, Interview, Task), AI-assisted software development, and AI-generated engineering documentation that I review and validate.

How I work

Sole technical lead between plant management, quality, production, and design. I run CAPEX projects from justification to production and build ROI cases for ownership.

Credentials

Lean Six Sigma Green Belt and Yellow Belt, 5S certified. Precision Machining diploma from Chattahoochee Technical College. Business Administration associate from Georgia Highlands College. Finishing my Industrial Engineering degree at Kennesaw State.

What I’m looking for

Manufacturing, Process, CI, IE, or Manufacturing Systems roles in Atlanta, remote, or hybrid, where floor credibility and building things both count.
