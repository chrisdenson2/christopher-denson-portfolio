About Me

The engineer behind the numbers

I'm Christopher Denson — a Senior Manufacturing and Process Engineer with 8+ years of hands-on experience leading process optimization, scrap reduction, custom fixture design, and digital transformation in high-precision manufacturing environments including aerospace (AS9100) and high-volume plastics machining.

My strength is turning shop-floor problems into scalable systems. I've delivered $240,000+ in documented annual savings through systematic root cause analysis, Lean Six Sigma DMAIC projects, and process standardization. I design and build custom fixtures. I implement SPC-based quality control systems. And when the commercial tools don't exist or cost too much, I build them from scratch — equally comfortable with concurrent async systems and feeds & speeds on a 5-axis HAAS.

I taught myself Python, Streamlit, FastAPI, SQL, and machine learning on the job — building a custom AI-powered production analytics platform (ProcessForge) and a deterministic Custom MES and Scheduling Engine now used daily on the shop floor. I replaced $6,000 in commercial warehouse software with a custom app I built myself. That combination of deep floor credibility and modern data tools is what I bring to every engagement.

Currently pursuing my Bachelor of Engineering in Industrial Engineering Technology at Kennesaw State University.
